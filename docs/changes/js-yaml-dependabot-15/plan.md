## Risks

- A newer patch of one of the six may be published before step 2 and resolve differently from the dry run. Accept any version outside the advisory range; the Proof checks still decide.
- A patch bump could change stylelint or ESLint output. The lint checks in `## Proof` catch it.
- No npm audit gate exists after this change, so new advisories stay invisible until Dependabot reports them (accepted by Etienne, see `## Design decisions`).
